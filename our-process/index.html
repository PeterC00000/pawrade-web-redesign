The questions people ask before they trust us.

How do I know Pawrade is legitimate?

Pet Media, Inc. has operated since 2003 and was named #2 fastest-growing Consumer Services company on the Inc. 5000. We have 18,917 verified reviews and a 4.7 on Trustpilot. Every payment runs through PetPay, Inc.

Is it safe to buy a puppy online?

Through us, yes. The FTC says to research the seller, see the animal and never pay by wire, gift card or app. Our process builds in all three.

How long from reserve to arrival?

Typically one to three weeks, depending on the puppy’s age, breeder acceptance, the vet exam and your travel option. Your Advocate gives you real dates.

Can I talk to the breeder?

Yes, by message center, phone, text and often a virtual meet and greet. We just ask that it happens through Pawrade so it’s on the record.

What if the vet finds something?

Travel is put on hold. The breeder and their vet address it, the puppy is re-examined, and your Advocate explains everything before you decide.

When is the breeder paid?

Not until you upload your delivery photo. If anyone claiming to be Pawrade asks you to pay another way, it is not us. Call (888) 729-8812.

What comes with every puppy?

The pre-travel exam and report, a signed health certificate, a 3-year Health Guarantee,* 1 month of MetLife,† Prescription Reimbursement, a named Advocate and the Puppy Guide.
