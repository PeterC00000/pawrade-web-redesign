Step 1 · Find

Find the one that fits your home.

Browse thousands of puppies from Pawrade breeders. Ask the breeder and a Pawrade puppy expert anything in one place, or call and text a real person every day.

Find: the details

  • Browse, favorite and ask questions on any listing. The breeder and Pawrade puppy associates reply together in one message center.
  • Prefer to talk? Call or text (888) 729-8812, daily 9AM–11PM EST. Phone, SMS or email, your choice.
  • A specialist matches breed, budget and timing to your household.
  • Every message is logged between you, the breeder and Pawrade, so nothing is off the record.

Step 2 · Reserve

Reserve with total confidence.

One secure checkout with flexible ways to pay. Your breeder is paid after your puppy arrives, so you can say yes without second-guessing.

Reserve: the details

  • Complete one short Buyer Questionnaire. It travels with every inquiry so breeders can match you well.
  • Reserve on PetPay.com with a card, Apple Pay, Google Pay, Klarna in 4 installments, or financing.
  • A person reviews every order before it is confirmed.
  • Your breeder is paid after you confirm your puppy’s arrival with a photo.

Step 3 · Prepare

A vet check-up before the trip.

A licensed veterinarian examines your puppy before travel, and your personal Advocate walks you through the results and gets you ready for arrival.

Prepare: the details

  • Your Customer Advocate locks in travel dates around your puppy’s age and exam, with updates by phone, SMS and email.
  • 3–5 days before travel, a licensed vet checks heart, lungs, gut, joints, eyes, ears, skin and vaccines, runs a fecal test and signs a health certificate.
  • Findings go into a Veterinary Health Report we review and explain to you. If anything needs attention, travel waits.

Step 4 · Travel

Your puppy never travels alone.

Choose pick-up, a ground nanny, a flight nanny or an in-cabin chaperone. You get updates the whole way and a photo moment at the door.

Travel: the details

  • Four ways home: free local pick-up, pampered ground delivery, standard air travel with a flight nanny, or Airpanion in-cabin.
  • Travel is billed once dates are set, with SMS and email updates the whole way.
  • Health records travel with your puppy, and a digital copy lives in your PetPay account.
  • Upload your delivery photo to confirm arrival and start your Health Guarantee.

Step 5 · Home

We’re still with you after day one.

Your puppy comes home with a 3-year Health Guarantee, extendable to age 10, plus a month of pet insurance. Our team is a call away whenever you have a question.

Home: the details

  • Call MetLife to activate your included month of pet insurance, with no waiting period.
  • Book a wellness exam within 72 hours and bring your puppy’s health records.
  • Year 1 is backed by your breeder and years 2–3 by PetPay. Extend to the 10th birthday at checkout.
  • Common arrival ailments are covered by our Prescription Reimbursement Program.

Typically one to three weeks from reserve to arrival. Puppies must be at least 8 weeks old before release. 3-year Health Guarantee included with every puppy; most breeds can extend to age 10 at checkout. MetLife: NH, NY, RI, SC and TN residents excluded.
